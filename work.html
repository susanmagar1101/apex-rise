The Brief

Safety Training People Can Practise.

The Logistic & Warehousing Company asked us for hazard training that is practical and interactive, with progress tracking so managers can see how each employee is getting on.

We answered with one system that has a public website, a training portal for employees and an admin portal for managers. Everything runs in the web browser, so staff do not need to install anything.

What We Delivered

Five Parts, One System.

The screenshots below come from the live system, using test accounts.

01

A public company website

A public website for the Logistic & Warehousing Company that introduces the business, explains its approach to workplace safety and leads staff into their training.

02

A six-level 3D hazard simulator

Employees explore a 3D warehouse in a 360 degree first-person view, right in the web browser, and click on the hazards they spot. Six levels hold 51 hazards in total, with a timer and a score for each level.

03

An employee training portal

Each employee signs in to a personal dashboard that shows the training levels, which ones are complete, how many hazards they have found and what to do next.

04

78 safety guides and a knowledge assessment

78 safety guides, each with a Quick Check to test understanding, and a knowledge assessment that checks what staff have learned.

05

An admin portal with reports

Managers add employees, follow their progress level by level and download the results as a CSV file or print a training report.
